Introduction & Instructions

This is a innovative project which can generate music with brainwave data.

To start the experience, open welcome.html using your browser.

In the beginning, the user will be asked to wear both the EEG headset and earphones.

Then, system will play several pieces of music which contain no lyrics.

During this process, system will collect your EEG data and analyse your mental status, like mood.

After that, system will generate a piece of music using the result of the calculation.
